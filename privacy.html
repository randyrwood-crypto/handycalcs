Handy Calculations — Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Handy Calculations is an offline engineering reference and calculator app. This policy explains, plainly, what user data the app collects — which is none.

Overview

Handy Calculations (the "App") is a reference and calculation tool for downhole and fishing-tool engineering, built for use in the field, in the shop, or offline. The short version: the App collects no information from you. It runs entirely on your device, keeps no account, and sends nothing over the network.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any of the following, or anything else:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number, physical address)
  • Account credentials — the App has no login or account system
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic data
  • Advertising identifiers, or any data used for advertising or tracking

Any values you type into a calculator (depths, weights, pressures, dimensions, and so on) stay on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere, and we have no way to see them.

How the App Works

All calculations run locally on your device using data you enter directly — there is no server, database, or cloud component of any kind. The App does not require an internet connection, make network requests, or integrate any third-party software development kits (SDKs), analytics tools, or advertising frameworks. Because the App never transmits data, there is no third-party service to disclose and nothing for us to secure beyond the device itself, which is protected by the operating system's own app sandboxing.

Children's Privacy

The App is a professional engineering reference tool and is not directed at children. In any case, because the App collects no information from any user, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of consent in your region).

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the App adds a feature that does collect data — the updated policy will be posted at this same page with a revised effective date above.
